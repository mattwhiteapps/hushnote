Common questions

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, transcription, speaker labels, summaries and answers all happen on your iPhone. The app only goes online to download its speech models. See the privacy policy for details.

Why does the first setup take a few minutes?

Hushnote downloads its speech model once, then prepares it for your particular iPhone. After that one-time step it's ready straight away every time. Keep the app open while it gets ready.

Why did transcription pause?

iOS can pause long transcriptions if the screen locks or you spend a while in another app. Keep Hushnote open until it finishes; the "Keep screen awake" setting helps. Anything unfinished carries on next time you open the app.

What does Pro include?

Every feature is free. Free notes are transcribed up to 2 minutes each; Pro transcribes recordings of any length and finishes your earlier 2-minute previews as soon as you upgrade. Pro is available monthly, yearly or as a one-off lifetime purchase.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?

Pro is restored automatically when you sign in with the same Apple Account. If it isn't, tap Restore purchases in Settings.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Hushnote Pro. Or use Manage subscription in Hushnote's Settings. The lifetime purchase never renews.

Why can't I see summaries or Ask?

These use Apple Intelligence, so they need an iPhone that supports it, running iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Transcription and speaker labels work on any iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Hushnote keeps mishearing a name. What can I do?

Add the correct spelling to the word dictionary in Settings to improve future transcripts, and use Find and replace from a note's menu to fix existing ones.
