Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

In short: Hushnote does not collect any personal data. Your recordings, transcripts, summaries and questions are processed and stored on your iPhone and are never sent to us or anyone else. The only thing the app downloads from the internet is its speech recognition models.

Who we are

Hushnote is made by Matt White, publishing as Built with Paws, in the United Kingdom. For anything about this policy or your data, email matt.white29@icloud.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you create in Hushnote is stored only in the app's storage on your device. This includes:

  • audio you record, and audio or video files you import
  • transcripts, speaker labels and any edits you make
  • summaries, key points and action items
  • questions you ask about a note, and their answers
  • folders, bookmarks, your word dictionary and your settings

Transcription uses OpenAI's Whisper model, run on your iPhone through Argmax's open-source WhisperKit. Speaker identification uses Argmax's open-source SpeakerKit, also on your iPhone. Summaries and answers use Apple Intelligence's on-device model. None of this processing happens on a server.

We have no servers that receive your content, no account system, and no way to see or recover anything you store in the app.

Data we collect

None. Hushnote contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and we do not collect usage data, identifiers, contact details, location or diagnostics through the app.

Internet connections the app makes

Hushnote connects to the internet only to download its models:

  • Speech recognition models (Whisper Tiny, Small or Large v3 Turbo), downloaded from Hugging Face when you first set up the app or choose a different model in Settings.
  • The speaker identification model, downloaded from Hugging Face the first time speakers are identified.

These downloads only fetch model files. No audio, text or personal information is sent. Like any website, Hugging Face receives standard connection details such as your IP address when the files are downloaded, which it handles under its own privacy policy. Once the models are on your iPhone, Hushnote works without an internet connection, including in Airplane Mode.

Permissions

  • Microphone: used only to record audio when you start a recording.
  • Speech recognition: used for the rough live preview while you record. Hushnote requires Apple's on-device recognition for this, so audio is not sent to Apple. You can turn the live preview off in Settings.
  • Notifications: optional, used to tell you when a transcription has finished. These notifications are created on your iPhone.

You can change these permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings app.

Purchases

Hushnote Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app checks with Apple to confirm whether you have Pro, using Apple's standard StoreKit system. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing and exports

Nothing leaves the app unless you choose to share it. When you export text, subtitles or audio, or copy a transcript, it goes wherever you send it through the iOS share sheet, and is then governed by that app or service.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Hushnote's data may be included in those backups, as with other apps. This is controlled by your iPhone's backup settings and protected by Apple. Hushnote does not sync your notes to iCloud or any other service.

Deleting your data

You can delete any note, or delete everything from Settings › Delete all notes and audio. You can also turn on "Delete audio after transcription" to keep only the text. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

If you contact us

If you email us for support or feedback, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply to you, and you can ask us to delete our correspondence at any time.

Children

Hushnote is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data from the app, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have emailed us, you can ask to see or delete that correspondence. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects how the app handles your data, we will also say so in the app's update notes.
